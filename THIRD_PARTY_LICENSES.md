# Third-party source attribution

The private lossless AV1 backend in `src/lossless_cdf.rs`,
`src/lossless_headers.rs`, and `src/lossless_tile.rs` is derived from the
`gamut-av1` 0.2 lossless intra implementation. That source is distributed
under the MIT OR Apache-2.0 license. The remaining `avifenc-rust` source is
distributed under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.
